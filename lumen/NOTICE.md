# What this licence covers, and what it does not

**This directory — `lumen/` — is MIT**: the scripts and the icon. Take them,
change them, ship them, sell them. There is no reason to hold on to any of it.

**Everything outside `lumen/` is the viewer, and it is LGPL 2.1** — inherited
from Firestorm and from Linden Lab, with their copyright notices left intact.

Three things it does **not** cover, and it is worth being exact about which:

## The viewer source is LGPL 2.1, not MIT

Lumen the viewer lives in
[cattencarter/lumen](https://github.com/cattencarter/lumen). It is
a derivative of the Phoenix Firestorm Viewer, which is a derivative of the
Second Life viewer, and both are under the **GNU Lesser General Public License,
version 2.1**. That is inherited, not chosen — a derivative cannot be
relicensed. `CHANGES-LUMEN.md` there states what was changed, as the LGPL
requires.

## The name and the icon are not given away

`assets/` holds the Lumen application icon. It and the name **Lumen** are
excluded from the MIT grant above.

This is the same line the Phoenix Firestorm Project draws, and this project only
exists because they drew it clearly: **the LGPL gives you the code, not the name
or the logo.** Build on this, fork it, improve it — and give it your own name and
your own icon, so that when something goes wrong the people who wrote *this* are
not the ones getting the support request.
